1. Replace the proposed landing-page framing in a **separate review branch** with the historical-to-modern noncitizen-treatment category direction.

## Why the pivot is proposed
The existing dictionary term has a documented historical relation to conditional fiscal treatment of noncitizens. The new direction may allow Isotely to become a reference identity in a global, law-related category rather than depend entirely on an abstract AI-identity analogy. This is a strategy choice, not proof that the legal market produces higher returns.

## Risks and unresolved questions
- Mark registrability, conflicts and descriptiveness in actual jurisdictions and offerings require legal clearance.
- Specialist legal assessment, source rights, freshness and costs for multi-jurisdiction records have not been completed.
- Demand for comparator-specific relation records and verified change events is unproven.
- Indexing of preliminary legal claims could mislead; web/publication unlock requires separate quality review.
- Existing PR #3 remains Draft; this proposal should not interfere with its safety audit.

## Explicit non-claims
No established commercial customers, recurring revenue, global legal coverage, industry standard, proof of exclusive category ownership, or current valuation.

## Exit and publication gates
- **Draft PR review**: confirm content, links, responsive presentation, source precision and governance hierarchy.
- **Human legal/mark clearance**: before public reliance or claiming marketable exclusivity.
- **Public release**: distinct owner-approved gate, with explicit records for \`robots.txt\`, \`noindex\`, sitemap, publication date and any DOI.
- **Commercial scale**: maintenance economics, rights audit, independent use and real buyer signals.
