# Isotely Reference — Candidate v0.3
**Status:** Working category reference, not a legal standard or public legal advice.
**Owner:** Sohadot
**Date:** 2026-10-24

## Brand and headline
**Isotely**
**Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

### Historical scope
Ancient Greek *isoteleia* concerned specified fiscal equality for some noncitizens. At Athens this included privileged exemptions from the \`metoikion\`; scholarly sources caution that the extent of any other privileges is uncertain. See University of Milan's [Lexicon of Citizenship](https://politeia.unimi.it/en/databases/lexicon-of-citizenship/), under Isoteles/isoteleia. Oxford's *isotely* dictionary record attests to the English word but is not a registration of contemporary ownership.

### Modern extension
Isotely's proposed contemporary framework asks when treatment of a **defined noncitizen status** is equivalent, conditioned, or different relative to a **defined comparator** for **one particular entitlement or obligation** in an identified jurisdiction on an effective date.

This is **not** a claim of universal equality without citizenship. It is an analysis of delimited rights, burdens and exceptions.

### Structured relation
\`jurisdiction | status | comparator | specific right/obligation | rule and exceptions | legal basis | effective date | verified as of | evidence/reviewer state\`

Assessment categories:
- equal under the specified rule;
- conditional equivalence;
- different legal foundation;
- different entitlement;
- inapplicable/explicitly excluded;
- unresolved/disputed.

Missing evidence must not be translated to an adverse or favorable finding.

### Example evidence
The Canada pilot comprises three **point-in-time** and **not lawyer-reviewed** comparisons on s.6 of the Canadian Charter, IRPA and federal electoral eligibility. They are not evidence of a verified legal change. Sources should be checked against their dated live counterparts before professional reuse.

### Strategic discipline
The descriptor “legal-status treatment comparison” remains generic. *Isotely* is a candidate commercial source identifier: avoid linguistic use that treats it as a generic common noun for any status comparison. Rights must be assessed in target jurisdictions.

### Business hypothesis
An attributable, accurately maintained relationship-level record may support research, licensing or change-impact analysis for legal and mobility products. Incumbents already provide law databases, comparisons and updates. The value to a buyer has to be demonstrated in defensible quality, customer utility, rights and renewability, not assumed from the historical word.
