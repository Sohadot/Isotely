# Isotely — Canada legal treatment pilot (research-only)

**Pilot date:** 2026-10-24  
**Jurisdiction:** Canada, federal  
**Defined status:** Canadian permanent resident (non-citizen)  
**Comparator:** Canadian citizen  
**Qualified legal review:** **NO**  
**Commercial publication approval:** **NO**  
**Verified legal change:** **NO**  
**Purpose:** test the proposed framework with bounded comparisons and transparent primary sources.

The cases do **not** establish comprehensive status equality. They do not address individual eligibility, case law in full, administrative practice or provincial licensing and local elections.

## ISO-LP-CA-001 — Pursuit of livelihood across provinces

- **Question:** Does Canadian Charter §6(2)(b) express a comparable right to pursue a livelihood in another province for a citizen and a permanent resident?
- **Working finding:** The provision expressly addresses both statuses for this stated right, subject to §§6(3) and 6(4).
- **Relationship:** equal on defined text, subject to stated qualifications.
- **Not established:** equivalent access to every occupation, regulated profession or benefit.
- **Primary source:** [Government of Canada — Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms §6](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-12.html).

## ISO-LP-CA-002 — Right to enter and remain

- **Question:** Do citizens and permanent residents possess entry/residence protection on the same legal basis?
- **Working finding:** Charter §6(1) guarantees citizens' entry and residence rights. IRPA §27(1) provides a statutory right for permanent residents, subject to IRPA including residence obligations in §28.
- **Relationship:** different legal basis; **not** a claim that permanent residents have no right.
- **Not established:** result for any particular removal, appeal or loss-of-status scenario.
- **Primary sources:** [Charter §6](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-12.html); [IRPA §27](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-2.5/section-27.html); [IRPA §28](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-2.5/section-28.html).

## ISO-LP-CA-003 — Federal electoral voting

- **Question:** May a non-citizen permanent resident vote in a Canadian federal election on the same basis as a citizen who otherwise meets voting requirements?
- **Working finding:** Federal voting eligibility requires Canadian citizenship; permanent residence alone does not suffice.
- **Relationship:** distinct legal treatment for the specified electoral right.
- **Not established:** provincial, municipal or other election eligibility rules.
- **Official source:** [Elections Canada — voting eligibility](https://www.elections.ca/content.aspx?section=vot&dir=reg/elig&document=index&lang=e).

## Evidence and interpretation controls
