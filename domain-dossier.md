## Strategic thesis
**Isotely — Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

Isotely is an attested word, with a documented historical lineage through the Greek *isoteleia* and *isoteles*. The historical practice was a specified privilege concerning fiscal equality for noncitizens. The modern proposed category examines **when the treatment of a defined noncitizen legal-status profile matches, differs from, or is conditioned relative to that of a precisely specified citizen comparator, for one right or obligation in a particular jurisdiction and at an effective time**.

The historical definition does not automatically encompass every modern legal field. The slogan is a contemporary editorial interpretation, not a claim of full citizenship-equivalent rights in classical Greece.

## Scope
Global by the design of the analytical relation; evidence only where legally sourced and scoped. Initial Canada pilot is a point-in-time research demonstration, not global coverage, legal advice, or proof of a change event.

### What Isotely is not
- General immigration directory, immigration service, or legal advice practice
- Citizenship-law database of every jurisdiction
- Simplistic country rankings or human-rights scores
- Automatically correct AI legal inference or unsupervised legal opinion generator
- Claimed first-ever legal comparison dataset, tracker, or notification service
- Established international standard, certified framework, registered mark, or existing revenue stream

## Category architecture
- **Historical word / brand:** Isotely
- **Public-facing editorial proposition:** Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.
- **Generic analytical descriptor:** legal-status treatment comparison (or legal-status treatment parity, with stated limitations)
- **Potential research product:** Isotely Reference
- **Potential data product:** Isotely Register
- **Potential verified-change product:** Isotely Parity Change Ledger

Modern category terms and future product names are working designations, not assertions of legal exclusivity, registration, endorsement, or market adoption.

## Unit of analysis
\`jurisdiction × precise noncitizen legal status × precise comparator × specific right/obligation × legally relevant conditions/exceptions × legal sources × effective time × review state\`.

We compare a **relationship**, not a country's intrinsic property. A right can be shared yet rest on different legal foundations; uncertainty and inapplicability are first-class findings. Generic citizenship eligibility and noncitizen status must not be conflated across jurisdictions.

## Evidence quality gate
A record is publishable as an authoritative legal finding only when:
1. It identifies both status categories and the entitlement precisely.
2. It pins each conclusion to current authoritative material.
3. It captures effective dates, hierarchy, conditions, geographic scope, and known exceptions.
4. An authorized and qualified legal reviewer has reviewed the relevant jurisdiction.
5. It does not infer equality/inequality from missing evidence.
6. Any alleged *change event* includes verified before and after snapshots and the operative instrument.
7. Its source and data rights are compatible with contemplated publication/licensing.
8. It is released under explicit owner-approved publication governance.

The three Canadian examples are **research only**; qualified review is pending; they are not proven legal change events.

## Competitive map / falsifiability
- ImmigSR has existing comparative historical migrant social-rights coverage across 45 countries.
- GLOBALCIT contains extensive comparative citizenship-law data.
- Commercial legal-mobility firms already publish legal updates and some employee-specific impact analysis.

The differentiation hypothesis is **source-verified, comparator-specific, right-specific treatment records with explicit uncertainty and verifiable temporal change**. This needs external customer evidence and competitive feature-by-feature testing. No novelty monopoly is presumed.

## Value model
**Propagation** can widen discovery; **attribution** can build reference authority; **reviewable implementation** can add operational leverage; **brand/domain** are potential scarce identity assets subject to actual control and transferable rights.

Possible future revenue: narrowly licensed data, compliant integration or subscriptions for verified changes, professional reuse. No verified revenue, profit, brand valuation, buyer interest, or six-/seven-figure price is claimed.

External scientific recognition is not equivalent to transferable commercial value. Tracking includes independent citations and qualified inbound interest *and* actual pricing, maintenance cost, renewal, rights provenance, and buyer-specific value.
