## Main expression
**Isotely — Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

The historical noun supplies a specific conceptual tension, not a generic “intelligence” software category. A qualified non-citizen may be equal to a citizen in a *bounded treatment* without becoming a citizen. Ancient \`isoteleia\` addressed fiscal privileges; the modern analytical category is **explicitly proposed**, not claimed as the original full dictionary meaning.

## Reference proposition
A comparison concerns a **defined status pair, a single legal treatment, jurisdiction, applicable conditions and evidence at a particular time**. Results may be equal, conditional, different in source, different in entitlement, inapplicable or unresolved. Unlike generalized migration information, the unit is a testable treatment relationship—not a country score. Commercial differentiation is a hypothesis to be demonstrated against existing legal-data products.
