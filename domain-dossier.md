## Executive position

**Isotely** is an attested rare word with a historical connection to Greek *isoteleia*, a bounded form of equal treatment of certain non-citizens, notably in civic fiscal privileges or obligations, without thereby conferring full citizenship.

**Brand line:** **Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

**Modern reference thesis:** clarify specific legal circumstances in which a non-citizen status receives treatment comparable to a citizen or declared reference status, and where exceptions, differing legal bases or exclusions apply.

This is a **proposed contemporary extension** of the ancient historical theme — not a claim that the original institution meant comprehensive equality, contemporary migration law or our particular reference methodology.

## Strategic identity

- **Geography:** global ambition; strictly selective and source-supported practical coverage
- **Primary editorial category:** historically grounded comparative treatment of non-citizens
- **Commercial mode:** reference-and-evidence-first; candidate licensable status-treatment records
- **Brand asset:** Isotely, distinguished from general terms for legal comparison
- **Business maturity:** research pilot; no verified recurring revenue or qualified buyer demand
- **Publication gate:** blocked

## Product and concept separation

1. **Isotely** — owned domain identity and prospective brand, subject to trademark clearance.
2. **Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.** — category's central question expressed editorially.
3. **The Isotely Reference** — definitions, limits, and sourced illustrations.
4. **The Isotely Register** — a candidate professionally reviewed, updated dataset, not yet commercially released.

The key unit is (jurisdiction × specific non-citizen status × comparator × precisely defined right/obligation × conditions × exception × authoritative provision × as-of/review time × evidence and review state).

## First reference pilot

Three research-only Canada records (federal context, 2026-10-24) explore livelihood mobility, entry and residence and federal voting. They are demonstrations of scoping; they are **not** expert-reviewed, not current-law guarantees, and do not establish a verified legal change.

## Risks and explicit refusals

- Cannot infer legal equality in general from a specific equal-treatment rule.
- Cannot treat different constitutional/statutory sources as evidence that a person has no right.
- Cannot claim that an ancient fiscal institution granted modern civil or political equality.
- Cannot imply trademark registration, exclusivity or seven-figure enterprise value.
- Cannot launch legal advice or a maintained global comparison product without competent legal review, update budget and rights clearance.
- Cannot use `isotely.com` as a live publication assertion until publishing infrastructure and authorization are confirmed.
- An existing technical AI identity research prototype does not establish legal service credibility.

## Intellectual and commercial thesis

Historical specificity creates a potentially credible conceptual identity; independent citations and primary legal records create authority; structured verified comparisons and maintained changes **might** create commercial licensing value. No buyer or acquisition hypothesis is elevated to a market fact without independent evidence.
