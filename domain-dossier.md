## Identity
- **Domain / intended brand:** Isotely.com / Isotely
- **Proposed leading line:** **Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**
- **Proposed worldwide category:** evidence-backed comparison of specific legal treatment for defined noncitizen statuses, against a defined comparator.
- **Descriptive term:** legal-status treatment parity.
- **Mission:** build concept authority and reusable commercial intelligence, with clean rights and evidence sufficient for potential eventual high-value acquisition.

## Historical root
The existing dictionary word is historically associated with the Greek *isoteleia*: a specific privilege for some noncitizens, including bounded fiscal equality, not an automatic grant of citizenship or every citizen's rights. Our modern method extends this historical distinction. Reference: https://politeia.unimi.it/en/databases/lexicon-of-citizenship/

## Analytical unit
`jurisdiction × noncitizen legal status × comparator × specific right or obligation × conditions × legal source × effective time × verification/review status`.

We measure a relation, not country rankings. An assessment may be equal under a stated rule, conditional, legally different, disputed or unresolved. Evidence uncertainty remains explicit.

## Commercial position (hypothesis)
Open reference authority, a maintained data ledger of comparisons, verifiable change records, professional data licensing and strategic transfer of brand and corpus are candidate levers. Competitors already publish migration/social-rights comparisons and immigration law updates; an advantage must be demonstrated, not declared.
