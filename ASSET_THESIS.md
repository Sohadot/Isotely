## Identity

**Isotely** is the exact brand, domain identity and prospective reference publisher.

**Primary editorial line:** **Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

**Historical anchor:** English *isotely*, associated with Ancient Greek *isoteleia*, concerns a historically bounded treatment of non-citizens, most prominently fiscal privileges or equivalences in ancient Athens. It **does not** mean universal equality across civic or political rights, full citizenship, or modern immigration law. The ancient root is a historical inspiration, not evidence that our modern method already existed.

**Contemporary working category:** *equal treatment across unequal citizenship and legal status*. This is a modern proposed analytic extension, **not** a verbatim dictionary definition or a claim to exclusive ownership of the underlying idea.

### What we do

Document, compare and, where independently verified, track when a specifically defined non-citizen legal status receives treatment equivalent to, different from, or conditionally convergent with an explicitly named reference status for a specified right, duty or legal effect.

The relation is always scoped to a specific jurisdiction, legal status, comparator, treatment, conditions and authoritative legal sources.

### What we do not claim

- All non-citizens have equal rights; ancient isoteleia conveyed citizenship.
- A global legal parity score or complete legal advice.
- Patentability, trademark exclusivity, a monopoly over a historical word or the method.
- Proven buyer demand, proprietary legal rights, a seven-figure valuation or verified change history.
- Legal review or current-law assurance for any pilot record.

**Global scope, selective coverage:** Jurisdictions are not limited to Europe. Launch coverage expands only with evidence, qualified review and a commercially sustainable maintenance path.

## Evidence unit

A record must identify: jurisdiction and level; as-of date; non-citizen status and qualification conditions; comparator status; narrowly identified right/obligation/effect; result (equal, conditional, distinct legal basis, unequal, or unresolved); exact authoritative citations and sections; material exceptions; reviewer status; uncertainty; verification time; and, if claiming change, independently established before/after baselines and effective date.

Missing evidence must result in **unresolved**, not inferred equality or inequality.

## Commercial thesis (unvalidated)

The potential business is reliable, maintained, licensable legal-treatment data and change intelligence for professional workflows that have recurring budgets. Public doctrine may build citation and discovery; controlled implementation, provenance, correction discipline and licensed records may produce defensibility.

No client willingness to pay, conversion rate, gross margin, market size or acquisition value has yet been demonstrated. Legal review costs, multi-jurisdiction coverage, competing data products and regulatory liability are principal commercial risks.

## Go/no-go gates

1. Brand clearance across relevant classes/jurisdictions before material launch commitments.
2. At least one qualified legal review of a constrained reference record with citations/conditions.
3. A genuinely documented before/after change, **only if** claiming change intelligence.
4. Product distinction relative to incumbent legal/mobility datasets established with evidence.
5. Costs and willingness-to-pay sufficiently tested before claiming commercial viability.
6. Publication quality gate explicitly passed before removing `noindex` or `robots.txt` block.
