## Canonical brand line

> **Isotely — Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

This is an *editorial proposition*, not a literal translation of every classical use and not a statement that all non-citizens have equal treatment. The capitalized **ISOTELY** is the intended source-identifying brand. The ordinary analytical terminology remains descriptive.

## Historical foundation — what the sources support

The Greek *isoteleia* / *isoteles* describes qualified fiscal equality privileges for certain non-citizens in ancient Greek civic settings. In Athens, the privileged metic did not thereby acquire full citizenship merely because fiscal obligations could be aligned with those of citizens. The historical scope was bounded and could vary across ancient polities. Do not claim that classical *isoteleia* itself meant a global framework of modern rights, immigration, nationality or human-rights parity.

## Modern extension — what we propose

**Working category:** legal-treatment relationships for defined non-citizen profiles, compared with a specifically chosen citizen or other legal-status comparator.

**Reference question:** When, where and subject to what legal conditions is a non-citizen given a comparable treatment, right or obligation without acquiring citizenship?

**Atomic record key:** jurisdiction × defined status × comparator × defined entitlement/obligation × applicable rules × effective date × verified source × review date × assessment/uncertainty.

Assess only within the specified rule. Possible outcomes include equal, conditionally equal, different legal bases, different entitlements, inapplicable and unresolved. This framework does not rank people, whole countries or the worth of legal statuses. Different legal foundations must not be misreported as an absence of rights.

## Category / brand / proposed products

1. **Isotely** — historical name, source identity, domain and stewardship.
2. **Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.** — editorial category banner, not a literal assertion of universal equivalence.
3. **The Isotely Reference** — conceptual definitions, limits, version history and public evidence.
4. **The Isotely Register** — candidate versioned dataset of status-to-status comparisons; not yet a product.
5. **The Isotely Change Ledger** — candidate verified before/after treatment events; no verified event claimed at this stage.

Terms 3–5 are working product names, not registered marks, accredited certifications or advertised services. The old AI identity work is preserved as a historical research track and must not silently be presented as part of the new legal database.

## Strategic geography and buyers

The reference is **global in potential scope**, but jurisdiction-specific in each actual record. It is not EU-only and does not assert global legal coverage. Target buyer hypotheses include legal information vendors, immigration/global-mobility platforms, law firms and researchers with demonstrable integration needs. None is a confirmed customer.

**Value thesis:** citable authority + source-specific maintained evidence + responsible rights and review governance + observable independent adoption + economically sustainable licensing may create a transferable category asset. A seven-figure acquisition is a stretch target, not an established valuation or probability. Avoid unsolicited outreach and public acquisition-for-sale framing unless the owner decides otherwise.
