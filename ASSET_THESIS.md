## 1. What Isotely stands for

Isotely is an attested English word with roots in ancient Greek *isoteleia*. Historically, this is linked to a specifically privileged fiscal relationship involving non-citizens; it was not the wholesale conferment of citizenship or every civic right. This historical meaning is the provenance of the name, not evidence that our proposed modern method already existed in antiquity.

**Public-facing proposition:** “Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.”

This is a deliberately concise *modern interpretive proposition*, not a literal historical dictionary translation and not a promise that all non-citizens receive equal treatment.

## 2. Proposed modern analytical reference

We propose a globally applicable **legal-treatment parity relation**: a documented comparison of how a defined non-citizen legal status is treated relative to a precisely stated legal-status comparator, concerning **one specified entitlement, obligation, exception, or protection**, in a particular jurisdiction and at a legally effective time, subject to relevant conditions, source hierarchy, and uncertainty.

The modern framework **does not** claim that:
- all non-citizens or all jurisdictions are alike;
- equal treatment is equivalent to citizenship;
- a country-wide parity score is valid;
- the research is an official legal standard or a service providing individual legal advice;
- this approach to legal data or change detection is unprecedented.

The status comparator is explicit. An unknown or unverified rule remains unresolved. A difference in legal foundation is not automatically a difference in practical entitlement.

## 3. Commercial asset architecture

- **Brand and citable reference:** ISOTELY identifies the source of a versioned reference framework.
- **Structured register (candidate):** granular, cited and reviewable comparisons, potentially licensable after suitable legal, rights and quality controls.
- **Change ledger (candidate):** verified before/after changes for specific relations, with effective dates and consequences. No verified before/after event exists in this proposal.
- **Acquisition value (hypothesis):** transferable, trusted source identity plus demonstrably useful datasets, documented QA, durable distribution, customers/licenses, or strategic buyer utility. Neither a six- nor seven-figure value is established.

The *generic analytical relation* and the *commercial mark* must be distinguished. Dictionary existence by itself confers neither trademark registration nor exclusive ownership over a historical word. Trademark clearance and appropriate service specifications are a formal gate.

## 4. Intended distribution model

Reference-first and inbound-first: high-quality searchable and citable materials after publication approval, independently auditable evidence, clear permissible licensing, and discoverable product enquiry pathways. No unapproved cold outreach or acquisition solicitation. No “domain for sale” copy.

## 5. Global without overclaiming coverage

The method is global by design, but coverage is limited to evidence actually produced and maintained. The initial Canada examples are **research-only point-in-time** exercises. They have neither independent qualified legal sign-off nor a demonstrated change event. Do not describe them as validated legal advice or comprehensive national coverage.

## 6. Competitive and market limits

Academic rights datasets, citizenship-law comparison projects, legal publishers, and commercial immigration alert providers exist. Isotely's potential differentiated feature is the **specific comparator–specific treatment–specific time** relation with auditable evidence and disciplined uncertainty. This is a **testable commercial hypothesis**, not a proven competitive advantage.
