# The Isotely Reference — Concept Definition

**Working version:** 0.3.0 · **Status:** proposed, unreviewed research reference.

## Lexical and historical foundation

*Isotely* is a rare English word related to Greek *isoteleia* and the historically bounded equal treatment of certain non-citizens, especially regarding civic fiscal obligations/privileges in ancient Athens. The ancient concept did **not** confer complete political or citizenship equality.

The modern use below is an explicit **extension**, not a historical quotation, a new discovery of ancient law, or a claim that Isotely is the only valid expression of the underlying thought.

## The governing question

> **Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

Under which specific legal conditions can a defined non-citizen status receive equal treatment relative to a defined citizen (or other stated reference) status **without acquiring that status**?

## Analytic relation

A record compares **(jurisdiction, source-status, comparator-status, defined treatment, conditions, exceptions, operative rule, effective/as-of date, evidence)**.

Results are not collapsed to yes/no:

- **Equivalent on defined question** — the same rule/effect is supported for both statuses, within stated exceptions.
- **Equivalent subject to conditions** — qualified similarity exists if specific legal criteria are met.
- **Different legal basis** — broadly similar effect may arise from different authorities, scope or stability; do not equate with no entitlement.
- **Different treatment** — rule-supported material distinction.
- **Unresolved** — insufficient, conflicting, outdated or unreviewed evidence.

Always separate **law-on-the-books**, legal interpretation, administrative practice and individualized outcome. A right appearing in a statute is not proof that all applicants receive the same practical result.

## Boundaries and editorial discipline

1. A record without an explicit comparator is not Isotely evidence.
2. A broad country score obscures the very comparison we seek.
3. Do not infer unrestricted citizenship rights from one equal treatment.
4. Do not infer absence of rights from absence of constitutional protection.
5. No claim of a legal change without an independently verified before/after.
6. No artificial implication that historic Greek isoteleia = today's entire status comparison apparatus.
7. No data resale or legal advice without provenance, usage rights and qualified review.

## Commercialization — clearly a proposal

The *Isotely Reference* could support searchable evidence cards, structured research licensing and a maintained *Isotely Register*. Buyers and economics remain unvalidated. We will test useful niche coverage and recurring demand before scaling or suggesting any company valuation.

**Brand:** Isotely is the source and potential commercial identifier. Avoid using it generically as the name of every comparison technique. Protection requires separate legal analysis.
