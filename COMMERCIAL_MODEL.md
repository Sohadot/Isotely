# Isotely — Commercial Model and Value Discipline

**Version:** 0.1 · 2026-10-25 · Proposed, not investment advice or a financial forecast.

## Commercial goal

Maximize the economic value of the **specific Isotely brand, domain, reference, and transferable assets** by creating a commercially useful, intellectually credible, citable category anchor. Ambition: a defensible six-figure asset value with seven figures as an upside scenario, **not a valuation claim**.

## A commercially legible buyer problem

International legal-status information is abundant but segmented by geography, status, and right. A prospective buyer may value documented answers to **how the same precise entitlement treats a defined non-citizen profile relative to a citizen comparator, in a specified place and at a specified time**. This is a *hypothesis requiring independent buyer evidence*, not observed demand.

## Value ladder

1. **Open reference:** defensible provenance, concepts, and selected properly cleared examples; supports attribution and independent discovery.
2. **Reviewed structured records:** properly licensed usage of documented treatment comparisons, with review status, temporal traceability and clear limitations.
3. **Maintained change intelligence:** verified before/after transitions and narrowly defined relevance, if one can sustain legal verification and source monitoring.
4. **Strategic acquisition:** a credible buyer could purchase transferable rights, code, customer relationships, contracts, maintenance workflow, or commercial advantage. Nothing currently establishes willingness to buy.

## Prospective customers (not signed customers)

- Global mobility and immigration compliance software platforms.
- Providers of legal information and research workflow software.
- B2B services dealing with cross-border resident or worker entitlements.

Commercial prospect selection should turn on specific paid workflows and legal risk appetite, not search traffic alone.

## Unit economics and gatekeeping

For each record, track:
- time to collect and normalize source documents;
- cost of qualified legal review and review frequency;
- interpretation and correction risk;
- change monitoring, translation, and rights licensing costs;
- per-customer integration, service and distribution costs;
- realized price and renewal behavior.

Do not scale jurisdiction count until **record accuracy, refresh cost, data rights and professional utility** are demonstrated. Avoid unsourced price targets, invented market shares, or probabilities of acquisition.

## Competition

Existing citizenship-law datasets, migrant-rights longitudinal datasets, immigration service providers and legal news products already offer pieces of this workflow. The narrow candidate differentiation is **comparator-specific, entitlement-specific, time-specific and explicitly uncertain** treatment intelligence. A controlled competitive test and buyer evidence determine whether it is worth paying for.

## Inbound-first distribution

After publication clearance, offer citable historical interpretation, a clearly delimited public example, provenance, versioning, documented reuse rights, and a professional inquiry path. Qualified inbound and independent citations matter more than self-generated page counts. No unsolicited outreach campaign is implicitly authorized. Do not display “for sale” or solicitation copy.

## Proof milestones

- **Proof of method:** at least one reproducible legal record, internally reviewed with explicit uncertainty.
- **Proof of professional trust:** independent qualified legal sign-off where findings are used commercially.
- **Proof of differentiated utility:** a reproducible workflow test against incumbent approaches on a buyer-relevant task.
- **Proof of demand:** genuine independent qualified inquiries or paid licenses with clear usage scope.
- **Proof of defensibility:** provenance, transferable rights, data hygiene, cost of updates, product reliability and contracts.

Without these, category prestige remains a thesis rather than a seven-figure business.

Proposed category pivot; branch-only demo; 3 non-reviewed Canadian point-in-time examples; no validated longitudinal parity change, no established paid product, no proven acquisition price, no claim of trademark clearance. The site remains noindexed until the publication gate clears.
