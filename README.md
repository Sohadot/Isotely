## Conceptual distinction
Isotely is an attested historical English word linked to ancient Greek *isoteleia*; scholarship documents defined fiscal privileges for certain noncitizens. The modern **proposed extension** concerns evidence-backed comparisons of legally defined noncitizen and comparator statuses, for specific rights and obligations at specific times. Ancient history is not represented as modern immigration law.
