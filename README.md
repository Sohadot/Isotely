# Isotely

**Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.**

**Current branch:** Proposed historical and commercial reorientation · **Not a production launch**.

Isotely starts from an existing rare English term related to ancient Greek *isoteleia*. The historical institution concerned bounded treatment of certain non-citizens, especially fiscal privileges/obligations, not complete citizenship equality. The contemporary work extends that *distinction* into clearly scoped comparisons of specified rights and duties across identified legal statuses.

This repository is stewarded by **Sohadot**. The proposed direction is **global**, not limited to the European Union; research coverage must remain narrow, sourced and professionally reviewed before claims are made publicly.
