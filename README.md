## Public-facing proposition

The phrase **Equal Treatment. Without Citizenship.** condenses the contemporary question Isotely is designed to investigate. It does *not* mean all non-citizens have all citizen rights, or that ancient *isoteleia* was full equality of legal status. The historically attested idea relates to specific privileges and fiscal treatment without conferring civic status.

## What the modern method compares

A **particular defined right or obligation** — not an entire country's record — between a non-citizen legal status and an expressly defined comparator, in a defined jurisdiction and time. The outcome must be evidence-linked, conditional where applicable, and explicitly uncertain when unresolved.
